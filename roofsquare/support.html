Common questions

How do I cancel Pro? Open iOS Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions > RoofSquare > Cancel Subscription. No phone call required, and all your past quotes stay readable.

How do homeowner deposits work? Paste your own Stripe, Square, or PayPal HTTPS payment link in RoofSquare Settings and tap "Test Payment Link". RoofSquare enforces your selected state cap (California: $1,000 or 10%, whichever is less) before opening your payment link.
