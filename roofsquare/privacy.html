RoofSquare Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 November 2026

RoofSquare is an offline-first roof calculator and quoting app for solo roofers. Your measurements and quote history stay on your device so you can read and export them anytime, even after cancelling a subscription.

What stays on your device

  • Roof plane dimensions, pitch, waste percentage, materials lists, roofer profile details, default shingle prices, and saved quotes are stored locally on your device.
  • Deleting the app removes all local data from your device.

Homeowner quote links and deposits

When you share a live homeowner quote link, the quote fields you choose to include (homeowner name, address, scope line items, capped deposit amount, and state deposit notice) are sent to our quote-link service so the homeowner always sees the latest version.

Homeowner deposits are paid directly through your own Stripe, Square, or PayPal HTTPS payment link. RoofSquare never processes homeowner card details or holds homeowner deposit funds.

Optional Sign in with Apple

Sign in with Apple is optional and never required to use the roof calculator or create quotes offline. If you choose to sign in with Apple, Apple shares an opaque user identifier for RoofSquare and (if you permit it on first sign-in) your name and email address (or Apple Private Relay address). That session is stored in the iOS Keychain on your device so you stay signed in offline without email-code lockouts, and your opaque Apple user ID is linked to RevenueCat solely to keep your Pro subscription tied to your account. You can sign out or disconnect your Apple Account anytime in Settings without losing your local quotes.

Subscriptions and RevenueCat

RoofSquare Pro is sold via Apple In-App Purchase. We use RevenueCat to verify your active Pro entitlement. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your Apple purchase and renewal status, device type, app version, storefront country, and (outside the EEA, UK, and Switzerland) non-financial funnel step labels (ob_step, ob_done, paywall_seen, ref_code). RevenueCat privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

If you request a refund through Apple, you agree that we may share information about your use of RoofSquare with Apple to help it decide the request.

App updates

When the app opens, it checks Expo Updates for newer JavaScript bundles. That request includes standard technical metadata (app version, runtime version, device platform), never your quotes or customer data.

Tracking and children

RoofSquare does not track you across apps or websites, shows no third-party ads, and does not sell personal data. It is not directed at children under 13.
