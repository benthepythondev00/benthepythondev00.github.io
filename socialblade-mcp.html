SocialBlade data in Claude and other MCP clients

Questions and answers about the SocialBlade creator-stats MCP server, from what it actually returns.

How do I use SocialBlade data in Claude or Cursor?

Connect the SocialBlade Creator Stats MCP server from https://mcpize.com/mcp/socialblade-creator-stats, then ask your assistant to call get_creator_stats with up to 10 creator profile links or @handles. Example prompt: 'Get SocialBlade stats for https://www.youtube.com/@mkbhd and show the last 15 days of subscribers.' It needs no SocialBlade account, login or cookies, and it works in Claude, Cursor, Windsurf and any client that speaks MCP over HTTP. It is an independent server and is not made or endorsed by Social Blade.

How much history do I get?

About 15 days of daily numbers per call, because that is what SocialBlade shows a logged-out visitor. The 7-day gain is normally filled in; the 30- and 90-day gains are usually empty. For a longer series, run the Apify Actor (https://apify.com/benthepythondev/socialblade-scraper) with 'Keep history across runs' (trackHistory) on a weekly schedule: each run adds the latest 15 days to a history stored in your Apify account, and the 30- and 90-day gains fill in once the kept history reaches that far back.

Which platforms are covered?

YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Twitch. Accepted links are youtube.com/@handle, youtube.com/channel/ID, tiktok.com/@user, instagram.com/user, twitch.tv/user, or any socialblade.com profile link. Bare handles are read on YouTube unless you set the platform. A second tool, get_top_creators, returns SocialBlade top lists by platform, and for YouTube by category and country.

What does get_creator_stats return?

Per creator: subscribers or followers, total views, video count, likes where the platform has them, SocialBlade grade and ranks, country and category, the daily history, the 7-, 30- and 90-day audience and view gains, and the dates of unusual daily jumps. Fields differ by platform, for example Twitch history can hold followers only. A field the platform does not show comes back empty, not invented.

What does a call cost compared with an Apify run?

On MCPize, every call goes through a plan: Free is 50 calls a month, Pro is $9 for 1,000 calls and Business is $29 for 5,000, and one call can read up to 10 creators. No Apify account is needed. Running the Apify Actor directly (https://apify.com/benthepythondev/socialblade-scraper) is pay per result at $0.003 per creator profile plus a $0.00005 start charge, on Apify's pricing. Use MCPize for quick lookups inside an assistant, and the Actor for bulk exports, schedules and the longer kept history.

When is the official Social Blade Business API the better choice?

Choose the official Business API (https://socialblade.com/developers) when you need first-party data under Social Blade's own terms, a contractual data source for a commercial product, or large volumes. It is sold as prepaid credits, one credit per profile lookup. This server reads the public pages a logged-out visitor sees, so it fits research and quick lookups inside an AI assistant, not products that need a licensed data source.

What does it not return?

No earnings or revenue estimates, no audience demographics or authenticity scores, no private analytics and nothing behind a Social Blade login or paid plan. It does not search for creators by topic (you supply the profile links, or use get_top_creators for top lists), and it does not run scheduled watches. History is limited to what the public page shows, about 15 days per call.

What are the limits per call?

At most 10 profiles per call and 50 records per target. A call takes roughly 4 to 40 seconds. Only what SocialBlade shows a logged-out visitor is returned. Rounded counts reported by a platform can repeat for a day or more, so a flat day does not always mean no growth.
