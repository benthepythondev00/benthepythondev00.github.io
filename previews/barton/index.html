How to tell your roof is due

Many people wait until the roof leaks. Most would rather replace it just before that point. Here is what to look for.

  • Its age. Cedar shake and composition roofs usually last 15 to 25 years. Metal and tile last much longer.
  • On a cedar shake roof, shakes blown off by the wind.
  • Water running down the face of your fascia instead of off the ends of the shakes.
