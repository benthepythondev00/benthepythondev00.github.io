Frequently asked questions

How can an agency pull YouTube and TikTok creator growth into Google Sheets without $200+/mo software?

Subscribe to the $29/month Agency Creator Stats Data Feed on Contra, enter your purchase email on the claim page to get your feed key, and paste the ready =IMPORTDATA(...) formula into Google Sheets. You get up to 500 successful creator lookups per month with no developer setup and no annual contract.

How does this compare to Modash, HypeAuditor, and Social Blade?

Modash ($199–$499/mo billed yearly) and HypeAuditor (~$299+/mo sales-gated) are full discovery suites built for larger teams. If your team already finds creators manually and just wants clean 7- and 30-day growth numbers in Google Sheets for vetting, this feed covers that workflow at $29/mo flat without split credit buckets or locked reports.

Does it detect fake followers?

No. Rather than claiming an opaque authenticity score, the feed flags dates where a creator's daily follower or subscriber change exceeded 3x their median daily change in spike_summary so your strategist can inspect that date directly.

What happens to my exported data if I cancel?

Every CSV file and Google Sheet you pull stays yours permanently. If you cancel mid-month, your feed key continues working through the end of your paid billing period.
