Privacy Policy for Welcome Path

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Who we are

Welcome Path is an app for Whop communities. It is built and run by Ben, a solo developer. When this policy says "we" or "us", it means Ben.

What we collect

Progress record. For each person who goes through a community's onboarding path, the app keeps one record with: the Whop business ID, the Whop user ID, the ID of the app experience, the time the person joined the path, the answers they typed to the owner's onboarding questions, which steps they ticked and when, and the time of the last reminder if one was sent.

Owner/admin flag. When someone opens the member view, we ask Whop whether that person is an owner or admin of the business. We save the answer (yes or no) on their progress record.

Billing record. For each business, we keep its Whop business ID, the date its Pro plan is paid until, the Whop membership ID, and the IDs and dates of payments received. We do not see or store card details. Whop handles payment.

Tip dismissal. If an owner closes the private-tab tip, that choice is saved only in their own browser's local storage and is never sent to us.

Server logs. Our web server keeps standard access logs (IP address, time, requested page) for 14 days. The app does not use analytics or advertising cookies.

Why we collect it

  • The progress record lets a member continue where they stopped and lets the community owner see how far members have got.
  • The owner/admin flag decides whether to show an owner the private-tab tip, and keeps an owner's own preview from counting as a member.
  • The billing record tells us whether a business has the Pro plan.
  • Logs are for keeping the service running and finding errors.

We do not use any of it for advertising or sell it.

Sharing

The community owner can see the progress of the members in their own community, including the answers those members typed. We share nothing else with other people. To check owner status and handle payments we use Whop, the platform the app runs on. Whop's own privacy policy covers how it handles your data.

The data is stored in a database on our own server, hosted with netcup in Germany.

How long we keep it

We keep a progress or billing record until you or the community owner asks us to delete it, or the business removes the app and asks for its data to be deleted. Server backups are kept for 14 days, so a deleted record disappears from them within that time. Access logs are kept for 14 days.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the data we hold about you, to delete it, or to answer any question about how we handle it. Email the address below with your Whop user ID or business ID. If you are in the EU or UK, you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Welcome Path is not meant for children. If you think a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the "Last updated" date.
