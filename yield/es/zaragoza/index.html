Zaragoza Benchmark Questions

What is the gross rental yield in Zaragoza in 2026?

Based on 100 asking sale listings and 100 rental listings captured from Idealista on 2026-11-24, the area-normalized gross rental yield in Zaragoza is 5.98% (€13.2/m² monthly rent divided by €2,654/m² sale price). Using unadjusted headline medians (€1,200/mo vs €281,500), the headline yield is 5.12%.

What is the median asking price per square meter in Zaragoza?

The median asking sale price in Zaragoza is €2,654/m² (interquartile range €2,194–€3,319/m² across 100 listings with verified floor area). The median total asking price is €281,500 for a median 108 m² home.

What is the estimated net rental yield in Zaragoza after Spain closing costs?

Applying Spain acquisition costs of 10.5% (ITP transfer tax (6–10% by autonomous community) + notary/registry (~1.5%)), 5.0% vacancy, 8.0% management, 8.0% maintenance, and 0.6% annual property tax reduces Zaragoza's 5.12% headline yield to an estimated 3.15% unlevered net yield.
