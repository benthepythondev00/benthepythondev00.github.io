Barcelona Benchmark Questions

What is the gross rental yield in Barcelona in 2026?

Based on 100 asking sale listings and 100 rental listings captured from Idealista on 2026-11-24, the area-normalized gross rental yield in Barcelona is 5.08% (€31.7/m² monthly rent divided by €7,481/m² sale price). Using unadjusted headline medians (€2,560/mo vs €1,195,000), the headline yield is 2.57%.

What is the median asking price per square meter in Barcelona?

The median asking sale price in Barcelona is €7,481/m² (interquartile range €6,422–€8,967/m² across 100 listings with verified floor area). The median total asking price is €1,195,000 for a median 142 m² home.

What is the estimated net rental yield in Barcelona after Spain closing costs?

Applying Spain acquisition costs of 10.5% (ITP transfer tax (6–10% by autonomous community) + notary/registry (~1.5%)), 5.0% vacancy, 8.0% management, 8.0% maintenance, and 0.6% annual property tax reduces Barcelona's 2.57% headline yield to an estimated 1.31% unlevered net yield. Adjust the calculator above for mortgage leverage or local tax brackets.
