Valencia Benchmark Questions

What is the gross rental yield in Valencia in 2026?

Based on 100 asking sale listings and 100 rental listings captured from Idealista on 2026-11-24, the area-normalized gross rental yield in Valencia is 4.78% (€19.0/m² monthly rent divided by €4,773/m² sale price). Using unadjusted headline medians (€1,700/mo vs €667,500), the headline yield is 3.06%.

What is the median asking price per square meter in Valencia?

The median asking sale price in Valencia is €4,773/m² (interquartile range €3,936–€6,000/m² across 100 listings with verified floor area). The median total asking price is €667,500 for a median 140 m² home.

What is the estimated net rental yield in Valencia after Spain closing costs?

Applying Spain acquisition costs of 10.5% (ITP transfer tax (6–10% by autonomous community) + notary/registry (~1.5%)), 5.0% vacancy, 8.0% management, 8.0% maintenance, and 0.6% annual property tax reduces Valencia's 3.06% headline yield to an estimated 1.66% unlevered net yield.
