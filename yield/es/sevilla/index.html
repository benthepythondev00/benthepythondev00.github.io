Seville Benchmark Questions

What is the gross rental yield in Seville in 2026?

Based on 100 asking sale listings and 100 rental listings captured from Idealista on 2026-11-24, the area-normalized gross rental yield in Seville is 4.06% (€13.3/m² monthly rent divided by €3,929/m² sale price). Using unadjusted headline medians (€1,000/mo vs €479,750), the headline yield is 2.50%.

What is the median asking price per square meter in Seville?

The median asking sale price in Seville is €3,929/m² (interquartile range €3,389–€4,890/m² across 100 listings with verified floor area). The median total asking price is €479,750 for a median 108 m² home.
