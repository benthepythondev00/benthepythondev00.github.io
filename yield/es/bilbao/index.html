Bilbao Benchmark Questions

What is the gross rental yield in Bilbao in 2026?

Based on 100 asking sale listings and 100 rental listings captured from Idealista on 2026-11-24, the area-normalized gross rental yield in Bilbao is 4.85% (€19.1/m² monthly rent divided by €4,715/m² sale price). Using unadjusted headline medians (€1,326/mo vs €497,000), the headline yield is 3.20%.

What is the median asking price per square meter in Bilbao?

The median asking sale price in Bilbao is €4,715/m² (interquartile range €3,676–€5,556/m² across 100 listings with verified floor area). The median total asking price is €497,000 for a median 109 m² home.
