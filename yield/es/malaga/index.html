Málaga Benchmark Questions

What is the gross rental yield in Málaga in 2026?

Based on 100 asking sale listings and 100 rental listings captured from Idealista on 2026-11-24, the area-normalized gross rental yield in Málaga is 4.16% (€17.8/m² monthly rent divided by €5,134/m² sale price). Using unadjusted headline medians (€1,400/mo vs €735,000), the headline yield is 2.29%.

What is the median asking price per square meter in Málaga?

The median asking sale price in Málaga is €5,134/m² (interquartile range €3,814–€6,639/m² across 100 listings with verified floor area). The median total asking price is €735,000 for a median 129 m² home.

What is the estimated net rental yield in Málaga after Spain closing costs?

Applying Spain acquisition costs of 10.5% (ITP transfer tax (6–10% by autonomous community) + notary/registry (~1.5%)), 5.0% vacancy, 8.0% management, 8.0% maintenance, and 0.6% annual property tax reduces Málaga's 2.29% headline yield to an estimated 1.11% unlevered net yield. Adjust the calculator above for mortgage leverage or local tax brackets.
