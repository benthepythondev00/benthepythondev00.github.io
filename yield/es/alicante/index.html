Alicante Benchmark Questions

What is the gross rental yield in Alicante in 2026?

Based on 100 asking sale listings and 100 rental listings captured from Idealista on 2026-11-24, the area-normalized gross rental yield in Alicante is 3.73% (€13.9/m² monthly rent divided by €4,479/m² sale price). Using unadjusted headline medians (€1,200/mo vs €822,000), the headline yield is 1.75%.

What is the median asking price per square meter in Alicante?

The median asking sale price in Alicante is €4,479/m² (interquartile range €3,268–€5,758/m² across 100 listings with verified floor area). The median total asking price is €822,000 for a median 230 m² home.

Applying Spain acquisition costs of 10.5% (ITP transfer tax (6–10% by autonomous community) + notary/registry (~1.5%)), 5.0% vacancy, 8.0% management, 8.0% maintenance, and 0.6% annual property tax reduces Alicante's 1.75% headline yield to an estimated 0.72% unlevered net yield.
