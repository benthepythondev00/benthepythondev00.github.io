Padua currently sits in cash-flow-oriented territory (rank #2 of 12 tracked IT markets), posting an area-normalized gross rental yield of 6.90% (1.38 percentage points above the 5.52% Italy directory median). Across 93 priced for-sale listings and 97 priced rentals captured on 2026-11-24 via Immobiliare.it, Padua asks a median €2,692/m² to buy (IQR €2,125–€3,438/m²) and €15.5/m² per month to rent (median €1,250/mo, price-to-rent ratio 26.3x). Immobiliare.it does not expose a native discount tag on Padua search results, so this 2026-11-24 snapshot establishes the 93-listing price baseline for automated 7-day and 30-day diff tracking. Padua Benchmark Questions

What is the gross rental yield in Padua in 2026?

Based on 93 asking sale listings and 97 rental listings captured from Immobiliare.it on 2026-11-24, the area-normalized gross rental yield in Padua is 6.90% (€15.5/m² monthly rent divided by €2,692/m² sale price). Using unadjusted headline medians (€1,250/mo vs €395,000), the headline yield is 3.80%.

What is the median asking price per square meter in Padua?

The median asking sale price in Padua is €2,692/m² (interquartile range €2,125–€3,438/m² across 93 listings with verified floor area). The median total asking price is €395,000 for a median 152 m² home.

What is the estimated net rental yield in Padua after Italy closing costs?

Applying Italy acquisition costs of 9.5% (Imposta di registro (9% second home on cadastral value) + notary/agency fees), 6.0% vacancy, 8.0% management, 8.0% maintenance, and 0.9% annual property tax reduces Padua's 3.80% headline yield to an estimated 1.92% unlevered net yield.
