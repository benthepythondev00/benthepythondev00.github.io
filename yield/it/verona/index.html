Verona Benchmark Questions

What is the gross rental yield in Verona in 2026?

Based on 99 asking sale listings and 100 rental listings captured from Immobiliare.it on 2026-11-24, the area-normalized gross rental yield in Verona is 4.61% (€14.2/m² monthly rent divided by €3,694/m² sale price). Using unadjusted headline medians (€1,175/mo vs €380,000), the headline yield is 3.71%.

What is the median asking price per square meter in Verona?

The median asking sale price in Verona is €3,694/m² (interquartile range €2,595–€4,423/m² across 99 listings with verified floor area). The median total asking price is €380,000 for a median 118 m² home.

What is the estimated net rental yield in Verona after Italy closing costs?

Applying Italy acquisition costs of 9.5% (Imposta di registro (9% second home on cadastral value) + notary/agency fees), 6.0% vacancy, 8.0% management, 8.0% maintenance, and 0.9% annual property tax reduces Verona's 3.71% headline yield to an estimated 1.85% unlevered net yield.
