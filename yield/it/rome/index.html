Rome Benchmark Questions

What is the gross rental yield in Rome in 2026?

Based on 100 asking sale listings and 97 rental listings captured from Immobiliare.it on 2026-11-24, the area-normalized gross rental yield in Rome is 7.33% (€25.6/m² monthly rent divided by €4,183/m² sale price). Using unadjusted headline medians (€2,600/mo vs €480,000), the headline yield is 6.50%.

What is the median asking price per square meter in Rome?

The median asking sale price in Rome is €4,183/m² (interquartile range €3,140–€6,100/m² across 100 listings with verified floor area). The median total asking price is €480,000 for a median 120 m² home.

What is the estimated net rental yield in Rome after Italy closing costs?

Applying Italy acquisition costs of 9.5% (Imposta di registro (9% second home on cadastral value) + notary/agency fees), 6.0% vacancy, 8.0% management, 8.0% maintenance, and 0.9% annual property tax reduces Rome's 6.50% headline yield to an estimated 3.87% unlevered net yield.
