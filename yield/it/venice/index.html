Venice Benchmark Questions

What is the gross rental yield in Venice in 2026?

Based on 93 asking sale listings and 96 rental listings captured from Immobiliare.it on 2026-11-24, the area-normalized gross rental yield in Venice is 5.88% (€20.0/m² monthly rent divided by €4,083/m² sale price). Using unadjusted headline medians (€1,950/mo vs €520,000), the headline yield is 4.50%.

What is the median asking price per square meter in Venice?

The median asking sale price in Venice is €4,083/m² (interquartile range €2,938–€5,948/m² across 93 listings with verified floor area). The median total asking price is €520,000 for a median 128 m² home.

What is the estimated net rental yield in Venice after Italy closing costs?

Applying Italy acquisition costs of 9.5% (Imposta di registro (9% second home on cadastral value) + notary/agency fees), 6.0% vacancy, 8.0% management, 8.0% maintenance, and 0.9% annual property tax reduces Venice's 4.50% headline yield to an estimated 2.42% unlevered net yield.
