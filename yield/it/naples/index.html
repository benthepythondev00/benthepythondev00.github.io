Naples Benchmark Questions

What is the gross rental yield in Naples in 2026?

Based on 99 asking sale listings and 97 rental listings captured from Immobiliare.it on 2026-11-24, the area-normalized gross rental yield in Naples is 5.59% (€17.8/m² monthly rent divided by €3,818/m² sale price). Using unadjusted headline medians (€1,300/mo vs €490,000), the headline yield is 3.18%.

What is the median asking price per square meter in Naples?

The median asking sale price in Naples is €3,818/m² (interquartile range €2,950–€5,200/m² across 99 listings with verified floor area). The median total asking price is €490,000 for a median 121 m² home.

What is the estimated net rental yield in Naples after Italy closing costs?

Applying Italy acquisition costs of 9.5% (Imposta di registro (9% second home on cadastral value) + notary/agency fees), 6.0% vacancy, 8.0% management, 8.0% maintenance, and 0.9% annual property tax reduces Naples's 3.18% headline yield to an estimated 1.47% unlevered net yield.
