Milan Benchmark Questions

What is the gross rental yield in Milan in 2026?

Based on 100 asking sale listings and 100 rental listings captured from Immobiliare.it on 2026-11-24, the area-normalized gross rental yield in Milan is 4.53% (€23.1/m² monthly rent divided by €6,111/m² sale price). Using unadjusted headline medians (€1,645/mo vs €456,500), the headline yield is 4.32%.

What is the median asking price per square meter in Milan?

The median asking sale price in Milan is €6,111/m² (interquartile range €4,583–€7,714/m² across 100 listings with verified floor area). The median total asking price is €456,500 for a median 82 m² home.

What is the estimated net rental yield in Milan after Italy closing costs?

Applying Italy acquisition costs of 9.5% (Imposta di registro (9% second home on cadastral value) + notary/agency fees), 6.0% vacancy, 8.0% management, 8.0% maintenance, and 0.9% annual property tax reduces Milan's 4.32% headline yield to an estimated 2.30% unlevered net yield.
