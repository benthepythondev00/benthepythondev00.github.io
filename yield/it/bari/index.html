Bari Benchmark Questions

What is the gross rental yield in Bari in 2026?

Based on 96 asking sale listings and 99 rental listings captured from Immobiliare.it on 2026-11-24, the area-normalized gross rental yield in Bari is 5.27% (€12.1/m² monthly rent divided by €2,748/m² sale price). Using unadjusted headline medians (€1,000/mo vs €310,000), the headline yield is 3.87%.

What is the median asking price per square meter in Bari?

The median asking sale price in Bari is €2,748/m² (interquartile range €2,101–€3,214/m² across 96 listings with verified floor area). The median total asking price is €310,000 for a median 116 m² home.

What is the estimated net rental yield in Bari after Italy closing costs?

Applying Italy acquisition costs of 9.5% (Imposta di registro (9% second home on cadastral value) + notary/agency fees), 6.0% vacancy, 8.0% management, 8.0% maintenance, and 0.9% annual property tax reduces Bari's 3.87% headline yield to an estimated 1.97% unlevered net yield.
