Turin Benchmark Questions

What is the gross rental yield in Turin in 2026?

Based on 100 asking sale listings and 100 rental listings captured from Immobiliare.it on 2026-11-24, the area-normalized gross rental yield in Turin is 6.87% (€13.8/m² monthly rent divided by €2,401/m² sale price). Using unadjusted headline medians (€1,050/mo vs €238,500), the headline yield is 5.28%.

What is the median asking price per square meter in Turin?

The median asking sale price in Turin is €2,401/m² (interquartile range €1,780–€3,386/m² across 100 listings with verified floor area). The median total asking price is €238,500 for a median 95 m² home.

What is the estimated net rental yield in Turin after Italy closing costs?

Applying Italy acquisition costs of 9.5% (Imposta di registro (9% second home on cadastral value) + notary/agency fees), 6.0% vacancy, 8.0% management, 8.0% maintenance, and 0.9% annual property tax reduces Turin's 5.28% headline yield to an estimated 2.99% unlevered net yield.
