Palermo Benchmark Questions

What is the gross rental yield in Palermo in 2026?

Based on 99 asking sale listings and 99 rental listings captured from Immobiliare.it on 2026-11-24, the area-normalized gross rental yield in Palermo is 5.94% (€10.0/m² monthly rent divided by €2,021/m² sale price). Using unadjusted headline medians (€850/mo vs €278,000), the headline yield is 3.67%.

What is the median asking price per square meter in Palermo?

The median asking sale price in Palermo is €2,021/m² (interquartile range €1,509–€2,667/m² across 99 listings with verified floor area). The median total asking price is €278,000 for a median 140 m² home.

What is the estimated net rental yield in Palermo after Italy closing costs?

Applying Italy acquisition costs of 9.5% (Imposta di registro (9% second home on cadastral value) + notary/agency fees), 6.0% vacancy, 8.0% management, 8.0% maintenance, and 0.9% annual property tax reduces Palermo's 3.67% headline yield to an estimated 1.82% unlevered net yield.
