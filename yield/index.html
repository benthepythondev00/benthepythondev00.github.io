Methodology & Questions

How are the EU and US rental yield benchmarks calculated?

Every city and district page computes area-normalized gross yield as (median monthly rent per m² × 12) / (median asking sale price per m²) from live Listingrail portal snapshots captured on 2026-11-24 (5,346 sale and 5,352 rental listings across 54 markets). US square-foot areas are converted to square meters using 0.092903 m²/sq ft so EU and US markets compare cleanly.

Which tracked city has the highest gross rental yield as of 2026-11-24?

In the 2026-11-24 snapshot, Chicago, IL (United States) leads the directory at 15.22% area-normalized gross yield ($3,154/m² sale vs $40.0/m² monthly rent), while Chamartín, Madrid posts the lowest gross yield at 3.63%.

Do I need an account to download the 25-row city CSV samples?

No. Every market page in this directory includes a direct, no-login 25-row sanitized CSV sample (13 sale and 12 rent listings) plus an interactive net-yield calculator pre-loaded with local closing costs. Full city datasets ($49) and live API feeds are available through Listingrail.

Why do some markets show 'Baseline' for 30-day price cuts or '—' for days on market?

We never fabricate missing fields. Idealista exposes active discount percentages directly and Imovirtual/Redfin expose listing dates or days-on-market; portals that omit those fields start with a documented baseline snapshot and populate 30-day price cuts automatically as weekly snapshots accumulate.
