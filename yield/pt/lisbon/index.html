Lisbon Benchmark Questions

What is the gross rental yield in Lisbon in 2026?

Based on 99 asking sale listings and 99 rental listings captured from Imovirtual on 2026-11-24, the area-normalized gross rental yield in Lisbon is 4.17% (€20.3/m² monthly rent divided by €5,846/m² sale price). Using unadjusted headline medians (€1,500/mo vs €518,000), the headline yield is 3.47%.

What is the median asking price per square meter in Lisbon?

The median asking sale price in Lisbon is €5,846/m² (interquartile range €4,427–€7,279/m² across 98 listings with verified floor area). The median total asking price is €518,000 for a median 96 m² home.

What is the estimated net rental yield in Lisbon after Portugal closing costs?

Applying Portugal acquisition costs of 7.5% (IMT progressive transfer tax + 0.8% Imposto do Selo + deed/registry), 5.0% vacancy, 8.0% management, 7.0% maintenance, and 0.4% annual property tax reduces Lisbon's 3.47% headline yield to an estimated 2.24% unlevered net yield.
