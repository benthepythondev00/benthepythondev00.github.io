Coimbra Benchmark Questions

What is the gross rental yield in Coimbra in 2026?

Based on 99 asking sale listings and 100 rental listings captured from Imovirtual on 2026-11-24, the area-normalized gross rental yield in Coimbra is 4.12% (€11.6/m² monthly rent divided by €3,377/m² sale price). Using unadjusted headline medians (€800/mo vs €310,000), the headline yield is 3.10%.

What is the median asking price per square meter in Coimbra?

The median asking sale price in Coimbra is €3,377/m² (interquartile range €2,606–€4,106/m² across 99 listings with verified floor area). The median total asking price is €310,000 for a median 92 m² home.
