Aveiro Benchmark Questions

What is the gross rental yield in Aveiro in 2026?

Based on 100 asking sale listings and 100 rental listings captured from Imovirtual on 2026-11-24, the area-normalized gross rental yield in Aveiro is 4.08% (€10.6/m² monthly rent divided by €3,108/m² sale price). Using unadjusted headline medians (€900/mo vs €310,000), the headline yield is 3.48%.

What is the median asking price per square meter in Aveiro?

The median asking sale price in Aveiro is €3,108/m² (interquartile range €2,391–€4,055/m² across 100 listings with verified floor area). The median total asking price is €310,000 for a median 104 m² home.

What is the estimated net rental yield in Aveiro after Portugal closing costs?

Applying Portugal acquisition costs of 7.5% (IMT progressive transfer tax + 0.8% Imposto do Selo + deed/registry), 5.0% vacancy, 8.0% management, 7.0% maintenance, and 0.4% annual property tax reduces Aveiro's 3.48% headline yield to an estimated 2.24% unlevered net yield.
