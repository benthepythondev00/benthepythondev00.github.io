Porto Benchmark Questions

What is the gross rental yield in Porto in 2026?

Based on 100 asking sale listings and 99 rental listings captured from Imovirtual on 2026-11-24, the area-normalized gross rental yield in Porto is 3.96% (€15.8/m² monthly rent divided by €4,796/m² sale price). Using unadjusted headline medians (€1,150/mo vs €400,000), the headline yield is 3.45%.

What is the median asking price per square meter in Porto?

The median asking sale price in Porto is €4,796/m² (interquartile range €3,895–€5,854/m² across 100 listings with verified floor area). The median total asking price is €400,000 for a median 96 m² home.

Applying Portugal acquisition costs of 7.5% (IMT progressive transfer tax + 0.8% Imposto do Selo + deed/registry), 5.0% vacancy, 8.0% management, 7.0% maintenance, and 0.4% annual property tax reduces Porto's 3.45% headline yield to an estimated 2.22% unlevered net yield.
