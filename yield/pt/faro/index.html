Faro Benchmark Questions

What is the gross rental yield in Faro in 2026?

Based on 94 asking sale listings and 100 rental listings captured from Imovirtual on 2026-11-24, the area-normalized gross rental yield in Faro is 3.68% (€17.2/m² monthly rent divided by €5,612/m² sale price). Using unadjusted headline medians (€1,250/mo vs €425,000), the headline yield is 3.53%.

What is the median asking price per square meter in Faro?

The median asking sale price in Faro is €5,612/m² (interquartile range €4,393–€6,426/m² across 94 listings with verified floor area). The median total asking price is €425,000 for a median 84 m² home.

Applying Portugal acquisition costs of 7.5% (IMT progressive transfer tax + 0.8% Imposto do Selo + deed/registry), 5.0% vacancy, 8.0% management, 7.0% maintenance, and 0.4% annual property tax reduces Faro's 3.53% headline yield to an estimated 2.28% unlevered net yield.
