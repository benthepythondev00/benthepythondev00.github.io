Funchal Benchmark Questions

What is the gross rental yield in Funchal in 2026?

Based on 97 asking sale listings and 75 rental listings captured from Imovirtual on 2026-11-24, the area-normalized gross rental yield in Funchal is 4.16% (€19.5/m² monthly rent divided by €5,634/m² sale price). Using unadjusted headline medians (€1,700/mo vs €545,000), the headline yield is 3.74%.

What is the median asking price per square meter in Funchal?

The median asking sale price in Funchal is €5,634/m² (interquartile range €4,477–€6,739/m² across 97 listings with verified floor area). The median total asking price is €545,000 for a median 105 m² home.
