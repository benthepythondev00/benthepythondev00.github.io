Braga Benchmark Questions

What is the gross rental yield in Braga in 2026?

Based on 98 asking sale listings and 100 rental listings captured from Imovirtual on 2026-11-24, the area-normalized gross rental yield in Braga is 4.40% (€10.9/m² monthly rent divided by €2,980/m² sale price). Using unadjusted headline medians (€950/mo vs €282,500), the headline yield is 4.04%.

What is the median asking price per square meter in Braga?

The median asking sale price in Braga is €2,980/m² (interquartile range €2,419–€3,629/m² across 98 listings with verified floor area). The median total asking price is €282,500 for a median 100 m² home.

Applying Portugal acquisition costs of 7.5% (IMT progressive transfer tax + 0.8% Imposto do Selo + deed/registry), 5.0% vacancy, 8.0% management, 7.0% maintenance, and 0.4% annual property tax reduces Braga's 4.04% headline yield to an estimated 2.66% unlevered net yield.
