How to Get Craigslist Real Estate Listings as Structured Data (FSBO, Rentals, Rooms)

29 Oct 2026 · benthepythondev

Craigslist carries a lot of housing listings that never show up on the big portals: homes sold by their owners, rentals posted by landlords and property managers, rooms and sublets. Reading them one at a time in a browser is slow, and the pages aren't built for export. The Craigslist Real Estate Scraper turns a Craigslist housing search into one structured row per listing. You can download the results as JSON, CSV or Excel, or call it as an API.

Looking for rentals from a portal instead? See the Zumper rental data guide.

What you get

Each listing comes back as one record with these fields:

  • url and post_id: the listing link and Craigslist's own ID for the post
  • title and body: the headline and the full description text
  • price, bedrooms, bathrooms, sqft: the numbers you'd filter on
  • location, latitude, longitude: the street location plus map coordinates
  • posting_date: when the listing went up, so you can spot fresh posts
  • photos: image URLs (up to 20 per listing)
  • attributes: Craigslist's tag list, such as "w/d in unit", "cats are OK", "carport", "wheelchair accessible"
  • by_owner: true for by-owner listings, false for agents or dealers
  • contact_available and reply_link: whether the post has contact options, and the Craigslist reply URL
  • housing_type: the raw housing summary line from the page
  • lead_score: a 0 to 100 score from a fixed, published formula
  • source and scraped_at: where and when the row was collected

You can check that score by hand. It's an agent listing (10 points), it has 6 photos (10) and contact is available (10), the price is under $100k (5), it's under 1,000 sq ft (3), and price, bedrooms, size and photos are all present (20). That adds up to 58.

Use cases

From the actor's README:

  1. FSBO lead generation for agents. Set listingType to owner and category to rea to list owners selling without an agent. Sort by lead_score so the listings with photos and a reply option come first.
  2. Off-market deals for investors. The same by-owner filter, combined with price limits and postalCode plus searchDistance, gives you a short list of direct sellers in one area.
  3. Rental benchmarking for property managers and landlords. Pull the apa category for a city and compare price, sqft, bedrooms and the attributes tags (laundry, parking, pets) across neighborhoods.
  4. Market research over time. Run the same search on a schedule and track how pricing and supply change across cities.

How to run it on Apify

Open the actor at apify.com/benthepythondev/craigslist-real-estate-scraper and fill in the input. There are two modes. search takes a city code (sfbay, newyork, losangeles, chicago and so on) and a category: apa for rentals, rea for sale, roo for rooms, sub for sublets or vac for vacation. direct_urls takes exact listing URLs instead.

A for-sale-by-owner search looks like this:

{
  "mode": "search",
  "city": "sfbay",
  "category": "rea",
  "listingType": "owner",
  "minPrice": 200000,
  "maxPrice": 900000,
  "minBedrooms": 2,
  "maxListings": 100,
  "includeLeadScore": true
}

Once a run finishes, you can view the dataset as a table or export it. Runs can also go on a schedule or feed Make, Zapier or n8n.

Pricing: $0.02 per returned listing, plus a $0.00005 actor-start event per GB of run memory. At the standard 512 MB setting, 10 listings cost about $0.20005 before account discounts.

Honest limits

  • Contact details are not extracted. Craigslist hides phone numbers behind "show contact info", and you'll see that placeholder in the body text. What you get is contact_available and the reply_link.
  • The lead score is tuned for homes for sale. Its price bands start at $100k, so monthly rents always land in the lowest band. That's why rental listings in the example score in the 50s. For rentals, use the score to compare rentals with each other, not with sale listings.
  • Some fields are raw. housing_type is the page's summary string as written (for example "/ 2br - 925ft2 -"), and location is often only a street address. A studio comes back with bedrooms set to 0.
  • Many rental posts come from managed buildings. In the example rows, by_owner is false and the body text is a property manager's marketing copy. If you only want individual owners, filter with listingType: owner.
  • Run size is capped. maxListings accepts 1 to 500 per run, and each listing returns at most 20 photos.
  • Coverage is only what's on Craigslist for the city and category you pick. Listings that get deleted or expire won't come back in later runs.
