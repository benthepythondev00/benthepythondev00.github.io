    Questions

    Who pays for Apify usage?

    You do, on your own Apify account, at Apify's rates. The sample run shows what one run costs before you schedule anything.

    Do I own the actor?

    Yes. It is delivered into your Apify account with its source code, input schema and README. If you tick the Store option, a generic version without your inputs, data or branding is also published on our Apify Store page, and the build costs $50 less.

    Which sites are out of scope?

    Pages behind a login you are not authorized to use, personal data you have no legal basis to collect, and sites whose terms forbid automated collection for your use case. The source and its terms are checked before you pay. Paid proxies or CAPTCHA services, when a site needs them, run on your account and are named in the quote.

    What happens when the site changes its layout?

    Production builds include 30 days of a daily check run that emails you when a run returns zero rows or a required field comes back empty, and the fix is included in that window. Apify's own daily test of public actors only checks that a run succeeds with some rows, so a run that quietly loses a field can pass it. After 30 days, fixes are quoted per change, or you can move to a managed feed.

    How is this different from the managed feeds?

    A managed feed is a monthly service where the data is delivered to you. This is a one-time build: you own the actor and run it yourself.
