Nashville, TN apartment concessions and net-effective rents

Snapshot November 26, 2026 · 100 Apartments.com properties (1,167 floor plans) and 100 Zumper listings · publicly advertised asking rents

In the November 26, 2026 snapshot of Nashville, TN, 100 Apartments.com properties and 100 Zumper listings advertised a price. 61% of the Apartments.com properties (61 of 100) advertised a concrete move-in concession such as weeks free or a dollar amount off; generic marketing taglines were not counted. Where the special was free time, the median offer was 8.7 weeks free and the largest was 15.2 weeks. The median one-bedroom floor plan asked $1,836 a month; spread over a 12-month lease with the advertised special, that is $1,550 net-effective, $285 less. Among properties with a priced special, the median saving was 16.7% of a year's rent. Bedroom medians are published for Studio, 1BR, 2BR, 3BR, each from at least 20 floor plans. These are publicly advertised asking rents and specials, not signed leases.

Method

Publicly advertised asking rents and move-in specials. Not signed lease transactions, not private rent-roll data, not pricing or legal advice.

Net-effective rent
asking rent x (1 - free months / 12) for weeks or months free; asking rent - dollar-off / 12 for dollar-off and look & lease; asking rent x (1 - pct x months / 12) for percent-off. Waived fees do not change rent.
Concession value
Largest concrete value in the advertised special ('up to', 'select units'), so the net-effective figure is the maximum advertised saving, not a guaranteed one.
Already-net rates
Specials saying the advertised rate already reflects the concession are counted as concessions but never discounted twice.
Concession share
Share of priced Apartments.com properties in the metro sample whose special matches the strict concession regex. Zumper has no specials field.
Bedroom medians
Published only from 20 or more floor plans. Floor plans with 2+ bedrooms advertised below 75% of the metro 1BR median, below the same property's cheapest 1BR, or named as student / co-living are excluded.
Rent cuts
A rent cut is the same listing id advertising a lower asking rent than its highest rent in the last 30 days. Shown only once 4 distinct ISO weeks of snapshots exist; window_label names the real span (4 weekly snapshots = a 3-week change, '30-day' from 28 days on).

Questions

How many Nashville, TN apartments are offering move-in specials right now?

In the November 26, 2026 snapshot, 61 of 100 priced Apartments.com properties in Nashville, TN (61%) advertised a concrete concession. Only specials with a value we can parse count, such as weeks free, months free, a dollar amount off or a look-and-lease deal.

What is the net-effective rent for a one-bedroom in Nashville, TN?

The median one-bedroom floor plan advertised $1,836 a month. Applying each property's advertised special over a 12-month lease gives a median net-effective rent of $1,550, from 504 floor plans. Specials marked 'up to' or 'select units' are taken at their largest value, so this is the most a renter could save.

How is net-effective rent calculated?

Net-effective rent spreads the value of the concession over the lease: asking rent times (1 minus free months divided by lease months). Weeks free convert to months at 52/12 weeks a month, and a dollar amount off is divided by the lease length. Waived fees are a one-time saving and do not change the monthly rent.

Are Nashville, TN landlords cutting asking rents?

We report 30-day asking-rent cuts only after four weekly snapshots of the same listing ids exist. 1 of 4 weeks are collected, so the first count appears with the snapshot of December 17, 2026. Until then this page shows no rent-cut figure.

Are these signed lease rents?

No. Every figure is a publicly advertised asking rent or move-in special on Apartments.com or Zumper on the snapshot date. It is not a signed lease, not private rent-roll data and not pricing or legal advice.
