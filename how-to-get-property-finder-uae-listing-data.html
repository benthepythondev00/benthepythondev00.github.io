How to get Property Finder listing data for Dubai and the UAE

9 Nov 2026 · benthepythondev

Property Finder (propertyfinder.ae) is a big property portal in the UAE. Copying listings off it by hand gets slow quickly. The Property Finder Scraper takes a list of UAE locations, such as "Dubai Marina", "Downtown Dubai" or "Abu Dhabi", plus a choice of buy or rent. It returns one structured row per listing. You can also paste a full Property Finder search URL with your own filters already applied, and it will scrape exactly those results.

What you get

Each listing comes back as one record with these fields:

  • Identity: id, reference, url and title
  • Price: price, currency (AED), price_period (sell for sales, yearly for rentals) and price_per_sqft
  • Size and layout: bedrooms, bathrooms, area_sqft and area_sqm
  • Type and status: property_type (apartment, villa, townhouse and so on), offering_type, furnished, completion_status and is_verified
  • Location: location_name, full address, community path, plus latitude and longitude for mapping
  • Agent: agent_name, agent_email and agent_languages
  • Brokerage: broker_name, broker_phone, broker_email and broker_address
  • Contact: contact_phone, contact_email and contact_whatsapp
  • Media and dates: main image, images_count and listed_date
  • Other: rera permit number, plus query, which records the location search that produced the row

Use cases

The README lists these uses:

  • Market research. Compare asking prices and supply by community, tower or emirate. price_per_sqft and the community path make it easy to group listings.
  • Lead generation. Build a list of active brokers and agents with their phone numbers and emails.
  • Price monitoring. Run it on a schedule and track how asking prices change over time.
  • Automation. Send new listings to Slack, Google Sheets or a CRM through Make, Zapier or n8n.

    • locations: emirate, community or building names. The actor matches them to the right Property Finder area for you.
    • purpose: buy or rent.
    • searchUrls: optional full Property Finder search result URLs.

You don't need a Property Finder account or API key.

Honest limits

  • Contact fields are often empty. In the real run above, agent_email and contact_email were blank in every row. Brokerage phone and email were filled in. The actor only returns contact details that Property Finder shows on the listing.
  • RERA can be blank. The rera field was empty in all three example rows, so don't count on getting a permit number for every listing.
  • Some numbers are stored as text. bedrooms and bathrooms are strings like "4", so convert them before you do any math.
  • WhatsApp numbers may be shared. All three example rows had the same contact_whatsapp value even though they came from two different brokerages. Check that field before you treat it as a direct line to an agent.
  • Prices are asking prices. They show what the listing says, not what the property actually sold or rented for.
  • UAE only. It covers propertyfinder.ae, not Property Finder sites in other countries.
  • Blocking can happen. The residential proxy option is there for that, and it costs extra on Apify.
