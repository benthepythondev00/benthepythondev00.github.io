New free page /jobs-in-denmark/: English openings in Denmark at SIRI's Fast-Track certified employers (989 on the 21-10-2026 list). We read SIRI's HTML table daily, map each company to a public job board (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, Teamtailor, Workable, Recruitee; 57 mapped), keep Danish-located English postings no older than 120 days (264 today), and check DKK salaries against the 2026 pay limit of DKK 552,000. The weekly alert is a coded mailto waitlist (WEB-DK-ALERT) until a paid Whop membership is approved.
