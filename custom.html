What happens after you email

  1. I check the source, required fields, likely volume, and operating cost.
  2. I run a small live probe and return two to ten real sample rows.
  3. You receive a fixed pilot scope with setup, monthly service, usage, limits, and exclusions separated.
  4. The monthly feed starts only after you accept the pilot output and schema.

Commercial boundaries

  • Apify platform, proxy, unblocker, and third-party API usage stays on your account unless the quote includes a stated cap.
  • Monthly pricing covers the named sources, fields, schedule, and destination. New sources or schema changes are separate work.
  • The operating target is 98% successful scheduled runs over 30 days, excluding upstream outages and customer credential failures.
  • Public or customer-authorized data only. This service does not include outbound campaigns, consent management, legal advice, or guaranteed email availability.
