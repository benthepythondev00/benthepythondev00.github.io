How this list is made

Every morning we read SIRI's list of certified companies and look up each company's public job board on Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, Teamtailor, Workable or Recruitee. A board counts only when it has jobs located in Denmark. We keep the postings whose text is in English and link each one to the employer's own page, where you apply.

Right now __N_MAPPED__ of the __N_COMPANIES__ certified companies have a board we can read. Many large employers run their careers site on Workday or SuccessFactors, which these feeds don't cover yet, so a company missing here may still be hiring. Search its name in SIRI's list, then check its own careers page.

The pay limit check

The pay limit track of the Fast-Track scheme needs a salary of at least __PAY_LIMIT__ a year in 2026, set every 1 January (SIRI, Fast-Track scheme). When a posting has a salary in Danish kroner, we turn it into a yearly figure (monthly pay times 12) and compare the lowest number with that line. Most Danish postings don't show a salary, so most jobs here carry no tag. The supplementary pay limit track needs __PAY_SUPP__, but only while unemployment is low and the job has been advertised on Jobnet and EURES first.

What certification means for you

A certified employer can apply for your permit itself with a power of attorney, and you can usually start work while SIRI handles it. It doesn't promise that a given role is open to applicants from abroad, and you still need to meet the track's conditions. Ask the recruiter early whether they'll use Fast-Track for the role.

Why only English postings

We check the posting's own words: a job written in Danish usually expects Danish at work. Short postings with very little text can be sorted wrongly, so read the original before you apply.
