EU & US Rental Yield Benchmarks

Free city and district benchmarks built from live sale and rental listings, refreshed from Listingrail snapshots.

Open the full yield directory

How is gross rental yield calculated here? For each market we take the median monthly asking rent per m² and the median asking sale price per m² from the same snapshot of live portal listings, multiply the rent by 12 and divide it by the sale price. Each market page also shows the median price per m², the price-to-rent ratio in years, how many listings already carry a price cut and the median size of that cut, plus a free 25-row CSV sample you can download without signing up.

Paid Telegram Memberships

Memberwall turns a Telegram channel or group into a paid membership, run entirely from a bot.

Open Memberwall

How do I charge for access to a Telegram channel? Add @MemberwallBot to the channel as an admin and set a price in Telegram Stars, or tell members where to pay you directly and approve each receipt with one tap. Paying members get a single-use invite link, receive renewal reminders three days and one day before their period ends, and are removed automatically when it lapses.

Memberwall charges a flat fee instead of a share of your sales, and it never holds member money: Stars land in your own channel or bot and direct payments land in your own account. It suits trading education, paid newsletters, courses, coaching and other paid communities that already live on Telegram.
